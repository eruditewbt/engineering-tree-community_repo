The Engineering Tree

Everyone starts with the trunk (foundations). Then you branch into domains that map to real fields and real opportunities.

Why This Exists

Traditional education often teaches theory without systems. Online learning often delivers fragments without structure. We combine structure with real-world building.

  • Build weekly. Ship proof of work.
  • Document what you learn. Teach forward.
  • Use projects to convert skills into opportunity.

What You Get

A community that behaves like a guild + lab + incubator: builders collaborating, mentors guiding, and projects compounding into a knowledge base.

  • Trunk foundations + clear branch paths.
  • Starter projects + weekly sprints.
  • Public reputation through builder profiles.
